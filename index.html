The name

Boostan reads three ways.

In English, a boost. In Persian, بوستان — a garden, an orchard. And to those who know it, Saadi's 1257 masterwork, written as the companion to the Divan. A house of brands rooted in the same canon.

Our mark is the cypress — سرو — the tree Persian poets gave to grace and endurance, the one that never bends. Our pattern is the چهارباغ, the four-quartered paradise garden. Structure and growth, held together.
